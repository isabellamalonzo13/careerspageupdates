Careers page proposal

What the current page lacks, what comes off it, and the order we propose for the rebuild.

hirebooth.com/careers · as at September 2026

What we don't have

Present on most of the six references. Absent on ours.

Hiring processThe steps, who a candidate meets, and the average time to offer. Nothing is published today. Xylos publishes four steps and a stated 2 to 3 week average.
Site locationsOur four sites with addresses, and whether each role is onsite, hybrid or remote. Emapta lists 16 Philippine offices.
B Corp beyond environmentWhat the certification covers for employees: pay, benefits, governance, community. One block with its own heading, focused on what certification means for employees and hiring, leading straight into benefits.
Our values on the pageThe five values as published on our mission and vision page. They are not on the careers page today.
Open roles on the pageA live count and department breakdown before the handoff to the ATS. Today the page links out cold.
Equal opportunity statementOn the page, next to the apply button. Today it is a footer link.

Nice to have

Raises the page from complete to competitive.

More employee storiesThree to five, across sites and job families, video where possible. One testimonial today.
Growth pathsInternal mobility, lateral transfers between accounts, and named promotion examples.
Numbers on benefitsOne figure per benefit card, retention included. The six cards are adjectives today.
Candidate FAQRewritten around real questions: pay, shifts, equipment, timeline, reapplying, applicant data.
Talent community signupOne field for visitors with no matching role.
Early-career routeA graduate or internal-applicant path. Culture Amp runs a named graduate program.
All four sites listedMakati, BGC, Cebu and Bogotá, each with its own card, so a candidate picks by commute. Culture Amp gives each of its eight hubs a card.
120+ countries in the headlineOur reach is the one number no competitor on this list can match. It belongs in the hero, not the footer.

B Corp block

One band, the way Culture Amp runs its promo banner: a person on the left, the headline in the middle, the copy and a link on the right. Ours carries the B Corp story about hiring and employees, sitting directly above benefits.

Certified on how we treat the people who work here

B Corp certification is independently verified, and one of the five areas it scores is Workers: your financial security, health and safety, wellbeing, career development and engagement. So how we pay, insure, train and promote our people is audited, not only promised.

Benefits block

The six categories stay. The headline, the B Corp card and the level of detail in each card are the decisions we need from People.

Headline"We believe in providing the best package" is a belief statement. Replace with what the block actually is: "What you get, beyond the salary." Keep "from day one" if it is true of every benefit. needs People
Premium HMO BenefitsThe headline says premium, the copy says standard. Name the HMO tier, whether dependents are covered, and from what day.
Holistic Employee ExperienceTop-tier, competitive, total wellness: three adjectives and no benefit. Either list what is in it or fold it into another card.
Learning & DevelopmentTypo in the live copy ("inn-house"). Name a program, a budget or a real promotion example.
Engagement ActivitiesWorks as is. Add the cadence and the number of volunteer days.
Purpose-Led CultureThis is the B Corp card. It moves up to become the block above the grid. The freed slot goes to the strongest claim we can prove, retention for example. needs People
Financial SolutionsSay what it is (salary advance, loans) and name the partner if we can.

What we remove

Great Place to WorkSection and badge. We are no longer affiliated, so the page currently misstates our certifications. Check every other badge on the site at the same time.
B Corp as a benefits cardIt becomes the B Corp block above the benefits grid instead of one card among six.
Blog posts blockThree posts sit between the FAQ and the closing CTA. They belong on Resources, not in a candidate's path to apply.
